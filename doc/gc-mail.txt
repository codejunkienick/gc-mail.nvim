*gc-mail.txt*  Mail Gas City mayors and read their mail, without leaving Neovim
*gc-mail*

Mail the current buffer to a Gas City mayor and read the mail sent to
you, without leaving the editor. Gas City is a small world of named
cities run by the `gc` command-line tool; everything addressed to you
is addressed to `human`. Nothing runs when the plugin loads; every
`gc` call runs in the background with an argument list, and the plugin
needs no other plugins. Requires Neovim 0.10 or newer and the `gc`
CLI on `PATH`.

==============================================================================
1. COMMANDS                                     *gc-mail-commands*

All seven commands work as soon as the directory is on the
runtimepath; |gc-mail-options| only adds mappings and background work.

:GcMail [to]                                    *:GcMail*
                Mail the current buffer. The first non-empty line is the
                subject, the whole buffer is the body. Without `to` the
                recipient is the mayor; with it, that address. Opens the
                city picker; choosing a city queues the message and then
                clears the buffer in one undo step.

:GcMailQueue                                    *:GcMailQueue*
                List the on-disk send queue: pending, sending and failed
                messages with their last error.

:GcMailbox                                      *:GcMailbox*
                Open the mailbox float: unread mail in every city, newest
                first, one line per message.

:GcMailPanel                                    *:GcMailPanel*
                Dock the same mailbox as a split below the current
                window. Toggles; the same key closes it.

:GcMailChat [city] [with]                       *:GcMailChat*
                Open a two-window conversation with `with` (default the
                mayor) in `city` (default the first city of the city
                list): the timeline beside the work and a compose box
                below it.

:GcMailChats                                    *:GcMailChats*
                List one line per conversation across every city, newest
                last message first.

:GcMailSearch {text}                            *:GcMailSearch*
                Search the mail history of every city for `text` as plain
                text, case-insensitively, in subject, body, sender and
                recipient.

==============================================================================
2. SETUP AND OPTIONS                            *gc-mail-options*

>lua
    require('gc_mail').setup({})
<
`setup()` with no arguments takes every default. It adds the normal-mode
mappings below, resumes the send queue after startup and preloads the
city list once with `gc cities --json`. Unless `watch` is false it also
starts the background watch. A mapping set to `false` maps nothing; the
matching command still works.

        keymap ............ '<Space>mm'  open the picker ~
        mailbox_keymap .... '<Space>mi'  open the mailbox ~
        panel_keymap ...... '<Space>mp'  toggle the mail panel ~
        chat_keymap ....... '<Space>mc'  open the last chat ~
        chats_keymap ...... '<Space>ml'  open the conversations list ~
        chat_city_key ..... 'C'           city picker in the timeline ~
        chat_send_keys .... {'<Space>ms'} send from the compose box ~

        state_dir ......... stdpath('state') .. '/gc-mail'
                            the queue lives in <state_dir>/queue/ ~
        retry_ms .......... {10000, 60000, 300000}
                            waits before each retry of a failed send ~
        subject_prefix .... ''  text put before every sent subject ~
        subject_suffix .... ''  text put after it ~
        watch ............. true  follow each city's event log ~
        watch_resync_ms ... 300000  recount unread mail this often (ms) ~
        panel_height ...... 10  rows of the mail panel ~
        panel_poll_ms ..... 60000  relist every city this often (ms) ~
        preview ........... true  show the preview pane beside lists ~
        events_rearm_ms ... 60000  retry a missing event log this often ~
        markdown .......... true  opened mail and threads are markdown ~
        history_days ...... 14  days of mail history kept per city ~
        chat_width ........ 60  columns of the chat window ~
        chat_compose_height  3  rows of the chat compose box ~
        chat_mark_read .... true  viewing marks conversation mail read ~
        chat_poll_ms ...... 60000  reload an open chat this often (ms) ~

`setup()` refuses a prefix or suffix with a control character, the two
together over 40 characters, a non-boolean `watch`, `preview`,
`markdown` or `chat_mark_read`, a `watch_resync_ms` under a minute, a
`panel_poll_ms`, `events_rearm_ms` or `chat_poll_ms` under 15 seconds, a
`panel_height`, `history_days`, `chat_width` or `chat_compose_height`
below its minimum, or a key option that is neither `false` nor a usable
mapping.

==============================================================================
3. SENDING MAIL                                 *gc-mail-sending*

Write the message in any buffer, then `<Space>mm` or `:GcMail [to]`.
The picker lists one city per line at once and refreshes in the
background; before the first answer it shows a loading line. The queue
takes the message first and only then is the buffer emptied, so a write
failure keeps the text. An empty buffer, one over 120 KiB, or one that
is not modifiable is refused. Sends run `gc --city <path> mail send`
with an argument list, oldest first, with retries.

Picker keys ~
        j/k or arrows ... move ~
        <CR> ............. send to the city under the cursor ~
        <Esc> or q ....... cancel ~

==============================================================================
4. MAILBOX                                      *gc-mail-mailbox*

`<Space>mi` or `:GcMailbox` opens a float at once, one line per
message: a mark column, a read column, city, sender, subject and age.
The title lists the keys. Each city is listed with
`gc --city <path> mail inbox human --json` in the background; a city
that arrives later is added when it answers. An answer the strict
decoder refuses changes nothing on screen. Read mail the mailbox kept
stays as a per-message file under <state_dir>/mailbox/ until archived
or deleted. `H` shows all mail including history; a second `H` shows
the usual unread set again.

Mailbox keys ~
        <CR> ............. open the message; marks it read ~
        r ................ reply in a new buffer ~
        t ................ open the thread, oldest first ~
        c ................ open the chat with the correspondent ~
        s ................ ask for text and search history ~
        x ................ mark or unmark the message ~
        X ................ clear every mark ~
        m ................ mark read ~
        u ................ mark unread ~
        a ................ archive (one call per city) ~
        d ................ delete, after a Yes/No question ~
        H ................ all mail on or off ~
        p ................ show or hide the preview pane ~
        R ................ list every city again ~
        q or <Esc> ....... close ~

`m`, `u`, `a` and `d` also run on a visual selection, else on every
marked message, else on the message under the cursor. An opened message keeps
`r`, `t`, `c`, `u`, `a`, `d` and `q` to close it.

==============================================================================
5. MAIL PANEL                                   *gc-mail-panel*

`<Space>mp` or `:GcMailPanel` docks the mailbox below the current
window as a fixed-height split, with the key hint in its statusline.
It is a second view of the same mailbox: lines, marks, all-mail mode
and kept copies match the float. Opening, refreshing or a mail arrival
never moves the cursor. `<CR>`, `r` and `t` open above the panel, `c`
opens the chat beside the docked window, and `q` closes the panel. `p`
shows or hides its preview pane. Closing the last panel stops its
watchers and timers.

==============================================================================
6. CHAT                                         *gc-mail-chat*

`<Space>mc` or `:GcMailChat [city] [with]` shows the conversation
oldest first with a compose box below it, following the end until you
scroll up. Your own pending sends render as queued, sending or failed
lines until the delivered message arrives. Unread mail to you is marked
read while viewed unless `chat_mark_read` is false; `u` keeps a message
unread until you leave the chat. The timeline statusline names the
correspondent, the send state, liveness, and unread and newer counts.

Chat keys ~
        <Space>ms ......... send the compose box (normal mode) ~
        <CR> .............. open the message under the cursor ~
        t ................. open its thread ~
        u ................. keep the message unread ~
        R ................. reload the history and re-render ~
        G ................. jump to the latest message ~
        q ................. close both chat windows ~
        <C-j> / <C-k> ..... move focus between timeline and compose ~
        ]m / [m ........... next / previous message ~
        C ................. pick a city and open its mayor chat ~

`<Space>ml` or `:GcMailChats` lists every conversation, newest last
message first, from the history cache, loading each city once. `<CR>`
opens the chat under the cursor; `q` and `<Esc>` close the list.

==============================================================================
7. SEARCH                                       *gc-mail-search*

`:GcMailSearch {text}`, or `s` in the mailbox and the panel (which
prompts for the text), looks through the cached history of every city;
a city not cached yet loads once first. Results open at once with a
searching line and fill in, one line per hit, newest first, or a
no-matches line.

Search keys ~
        <CR> .............. show the hit as a message view (marks nothing) ~
        c ................. open the chat with the hit's correspondent ~
        q or <Esc> ....... close ~

==============================================================================
8. SEND QUEUE                                   *gc-mail-queue*

Unsent mail waits as one JSON file per message in
<state_dir>/queue/, so it survives a restart. One worker sends oldest
first, one `gc` process at a time; a failed send retries, then is marked
failed and waits for `r`. `setup()` resumes the queue; several Neovims
may share it under a file lock. Past 100 files a new message is
refused.

Queue keys ~
        r ................. send the message under the cursor again now ~
        o ................. open its stored text in a new buffer ~
        d ................. delete it (never while it is sending) ~
        <Esc> or q ....... close ~
